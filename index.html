Our story

1927 to 2027. A century of one family's freight.

1927

Fred J. and Myrtle Hermann start Hermann Forwarding in New Brunswick, NJ, hauling watermelons for the Great A&P.

1930s

Dedicated carrier service for Union Carbide and Johnson & Johnson.

1946

Fred J. Hermann becomes Mayor of North Brunswick and serves 18 years.

1950s

Two-way radio puts drivers in direct touch with the terminal.

1959

Construction begins on the South Brunswick facilities.

1960s

The second generation launches the Leasing and Warehouse divisions.

2004

Hermann Cares begins with a Toys for Tots partnership.

2022

The South Brunswick facilities reopen after a full remodel.

2025

New Jersey's largest electric commercial truck fleet.

100Years in 2027
2027

One hundred years. The fourth generation is still at the wheel.
